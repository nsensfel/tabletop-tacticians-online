Asynchronous Online Board Game Simulator

Rooms have lifetime, reset by update.
Similar to airboardgame, except:
- Edition mode and play mode are fully separate.
- Action history and replay (TO's puppeteer).
- More permissions on items, and they are handled server-side:
	- Items in Fully Hidden Area give no info to players not owning area.
	- Items in Hand Area only show their backside to other players, but show their
		front to the owner.
	- Translucent overlays show on the items to indicate visibility.
	- Putting items in there automatically claims them, unless a config is set
	for the zone.
- Server side shuffling.
- Server side dire rolling.
- Zone collapsing / tabs.
- Fewer item types:
	- Image: front/back.
	- Card: front/back.
	- Dice: 1+ pictures. May have its side manually selected, or server-decided.
	- Area.
	- Finite Bag: a deck. Draw from top, draw from bottom, put on top, put on bottom, put at random, shuffle.
	- Open Bag: take random, or look inside and pick a specific element.
	- Magic Bag: generates/deletes on the fly.
- Layers within layers: Should be able to put an object on another, even if
  they're the same type.
- Accounts.
- Open rooms looking for players.
- Rooms with restrictions.
- Snapping.

Player in a room:
- Can freely move their pieces. Must confirm once all moves are done to commit,
  in order to avoid filling the history with small moves.
- Cannot move pieces owned by others.
- Can freely move unclaimed pieces.
- Can claim pieces.
- Can change active tab in own area. This is local only.
- Can manually select a dice's side.
- Can request dice roll.
- Can draw from an owned / unclaimed finite bag (top, bottom, random).
- Can shuffle a finite bag (top, bottom, random).
- Can add to a magic bag.
- Can take from a magic bag.


Issues with decks and cards:
- Players will expect that moving a card unto another component causes that
  card to lay on top of the component. That's not trivial to do. Cards may lay
  on multiple components, too. Still held only by one, I guess? But the z-index
  should work.
- Handling discard and replenishing a deck.

Handling edition is also tricky, when you want to edit multiple objects at the
same time. Maybe keep the editor offline?

Disk usage is likely high: AirBoardGame JSON Heirless with bad compression is ~500Ko.

User history update on view vs on act: maybe a user setting?
